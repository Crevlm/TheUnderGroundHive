## Texturing & Lighting

### Immersion and theme

One of my goals when texturing The Hive was to make it grungy and dark to embody this sort of dark biker bar aesthetic.The aim was to have it dark and sort of abandoned feeling with inlaid lighting
<img width="1648" height="1092" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c4bb7a8a-7312-4c3d-a2b5-365b339f1bf0" />


I went with more stone, gravel, and wood textures to mimic the real materials that would likely be used in a place like this. I incorporated some more 'quake' -esque textures such as the more sci-fi style lighting on the outdoor stage to bring a little bit of a unique spin on it. 

<img width="2528" height="1310" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a971ad66-f68f-4d3b-8df9-d23a8e592814" />


### Guidance and orientation

I made an effort to allow the light to sort of guide the player but also allow them some areas to sneak around. I lit up most of the doorways to signal that the space continues.
